# AI Resume Analyzer - Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Project Overview

AI Resume Analyzer is an AI-powered web application that helps job seekers improve their resumes, compare them against job descriptions, generate ATS scores, identify missing skills, rewrite resumes, create cover letters, and prepare for interviews using multiple AI models.

---

## Problem Statement

Many candidates submit resumes that fail Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) or do not match job descriptions effectively. They often don't know what to improve before applying. This platform provides AI-driven feedback to increase their chances of getting interviews.

---

## Target Users

- Students
- Freshers
- Experienced Software Engineers
- Career Switchers
- Recruiters (Future)

---

## Project Goals

- Improve resume quality
- Increase ATS score
- Help users match resumes with job descriptions
- Prepare users for interviews
